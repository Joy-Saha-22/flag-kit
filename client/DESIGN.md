DESIGN RULES (strict, every screen)
Visual direction: a dense, quiet, tool-like admin interface, closer to a developer's internal console than a marketing site. Flat surfaces, thin 1px borders, careful alignment, readable tables.
- Color: page background #F3F2EE, surfaces #EAE8E1. Never pure white anywhere (no #fff, #ffffff, rgb(255,255,255), bg-white, text-white). Text on the accent uses an off-white token (#F3F2EE) named on-accent. Text #1C1C1A, borders #D3D0C6. ONE accent, deep teal #1F5F5B, for primary buttons, links and the selected state only. Status colors only where meaning needs them (on/off, significant or not): muted green #3F6B4A, muted red #9A3B32, muted amber #8A6A1F. Keep text contrast at WCAG AA or better and darken a color if it falls short. No gradients, no rainbow or multi-color charts (shades of the accent plus one neutral grey), no neon, no pastels, no purple and black scheme, no radial orbs, no dot grids, no glow, no glass, liquid glass or blur effects.
- Shape and depth: corner radius 2px everywhere, no pills, no soft rounded corners. No drop shadows (override Tailwind's shadow and radius scales so they cannot appear). Separate areas with borders and spacing. No colored left stripe on cards or alerts. No bento grids. No row of three feature cards.
- Type: IBM Plex Sans for UI, IBM Plex Mono for flag keys, SDK keys, JSON and numbers in tables, self-hosted through @fontsource. Never Inter, Geist or Space Grotesk. Two weights only (400 and 600).
- Icons: prefer text labels. When an icon is needed use @tabler/icons-react at one size and stroke width. No Lucide, no emojis, no sparkle icons, no animated arrows, no checkmark bullet lists.
- Motion: none. No hover animations, transitions, transforms, scroll effects or animated charts. Hover and focus change color instantly, and keyboard focus rings stay clearly visible. Center dialogs with fixed positioning and flex, not translate transforms. No skeleton loaders: show a plain "Loading..." line in the area being loaded.
- Copy: no em dashes anywhere in UI text, no marketing phrasing, no "not X, it is Y" constructions, no fake testimonials, no placeholder logos, no terminal window mockups. Plain, specific labels ("Enable in production", "Add rule").
- Pages: no pricing tiers, no Terms of Service or Privacy Policy pages, no landing page or hero. The first screen after login is the flags list. Show the real product only: no mock data, screenshots or faked demos.
